Questions

How does the free trial work?

New users get 14 days of Pro for free, with no card required. When the trial ends you go back to the Free plan unless you decide to subscribe. You're never charged automatically for the trial.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from Manage subscription in the app menu. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for, then your account moves to the Free plan. Your data is not deleted.

Do you offer refunds?

Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Monthly payments are not refundable, but you can cancel anytime to stop future charges. Your legal consumer rights always apply. See our Terms and Conditions for details.

How are payments handled?

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders and handles payments, taxes and invoices, and provides all customer service inquiries related to billing. We never see or store your card details.

Which currencies and taxes?

Prices are in US dollars. At checkout you may see the price in your local currency, and taxes such as VAT may be added depending on your country. The final price is always shown before you pay.

What happens to my data if I go back to Free?

Nothing is deleted. You keep all your notes, recordings, photos and projects — you just can't add new ones beyond the Free plan's limits until you upgrade again.
